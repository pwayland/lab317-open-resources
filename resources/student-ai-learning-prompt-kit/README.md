# Student AI Learning and Assignment Prompt Kit

- [English version](student-ai-learning-prompt-kit.md)
- [Versión en español](student-ai-learning-prompt-kit-es.md)

## Purpose

This bilingual collection gives students reusable prompts for learning with an AI chatbot. It covers explanations, study guides, study planning, practice questions, Socratic tutoring, assignment and rubric analysis, outlining, draft feedback, and final submission audits. It also includes clearly labeled prompts for complete AI-generated assignments, restricted to activities where the instructor explicitly permits that level of AI assistance.

## Intended audience

University students learning to use AI deliberately and responsibly, and instructors who want practical examples they can adapt to their own course policies and learning goals.

## Origin and current use

Paul Wayland created the kit for the *Data Structures and Blockchain* course in the Master of Science in *Logistics and Technology* at Inter American University of Puerto Rico, Metropolitan Campus, during the October/November 2026 bimester. It is now part of all of Professor Wayland's courses.

The Spanish version began as a Gemini translation and was reviewed to preserve the English version's structure, placeholders, and meaning.

## Use notes

Students should follow the AI policy for their course and assignment, avoid sharing private or restricted material, verify AI-generated claims and citations, and disclose AI use when required. The source files are plain Markdown so instructors can adapt the collection to different disciplines and policies.
