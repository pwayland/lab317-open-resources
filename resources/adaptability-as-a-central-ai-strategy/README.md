# Adaptability as a Central AI Strategy

[Download the presentation](adaptability-as-a-central-ai-strategy.pdf)

## Purpose

This presentation argues that adopting AI is not, by itself, an economic strategy. It connects Puerto Rico's economic conditions, uneven private-sector diffusion, rapidly changing AI capabilities, fragmented governance, and the role of education to propose adaptability as the central strategy. It closes with practical directions for public governance, organizational redesign, and education.

## Intended audience

Public officials, business and nonprofit leaders, educators, researchers, workforce-development professionals, and others interested in Puerto Rico's economic and institutional response to AI.
